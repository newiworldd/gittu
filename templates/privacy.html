Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Anik X Cheats Ticket Bot and Dashboard, we collect the following information:

  • Discord Data: Your Discord User ID, Username, Avatar, and Guild (Server) configurations.
  • Ticket Data: Settings related to your ticket panel configurations, welcome messages, and DM settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide the core functionality of the Anik X Cheats bot. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Server configurations are securely stored to ensure the bot functions correctly in your servers.

3. Data Storage and Security

We use MongoDB Atlas to securely store server configurations. We do not store local logs of your private messages or sensitive data outside of what is necessary for the bot's features.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please join our Support Server or contact an administrator.
